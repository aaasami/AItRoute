Why Beta Needs
to Be Personal

When climbers get stuck on a route, beta advice often comes from someone with a different body. A move that's "easy" for a 6ft climber may be physically impossible for someone shorter.

"I kept watching the same beta videos but my arms just couldn't reach that way. I needed someone to tell me my version of the move."

AltRoute uses your body profile — height, wingspan, flexibility — to generate step-by-step movement sequences tailored specifically to you.

Body-Adaptive Beta

Movement strategies based on your reach, height, and physical constraints — not a hypothetical average climber.

In-Gym Speed

Designed for the short window between attempts. Fast input, fast output — no walls of text mid-session.

Route Recognition

Identify gym routes and pull community beta, or generate new AI-powered sequences on the spot.

Community Beta

Browse video beta shared by climbers with similar body types. Learn from people who climb like you.
