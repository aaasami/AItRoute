CS185 HCI COURSE PROJECT

AltRoute: Personalized Beta for Indoor Climbers

Different bodies need different movement strategies, even on the same route.

AltRoute helps indoor climbers quickly generate and follow beta adapted to body profile, reach, and physical constraints. We designed this product for climbers who get stuck between attempts and need fast, understandable guidance in the gym.

Project Overview

Problem Space, Users, and Product Concept

Target Users

Indoor climbers, especially beginner-to-intermediate users, who need quick route support during active sessions.

Problem Space

Existing beta often assumes one ideal body and movement style. Many climbers cannot apply it directly, causing frustration and repeated failed attempts.

Product Concept

Capture route context, combine body profile inputs, and produce stepwise, personalized beta that users can follow in real time.

Design Process

From Research to Iteration

Needfinding

Interviewed climbers and observed in-gym behavior to identify critical friction moments.

Research Insights

Synthesized repeated patterns around body mismatch, confidence loss, and route uncertainty.

POVs & HMWs

Converted findings into actionable opportunity statements and design prompts.

Brainstorming

Explored divergent concepts for guidance systems, body adaptation, and interaction flow.

Sketching & Exploration

Compared multiple information architectures and screened for speed and clarity.

Low-Fi Prototype

Validated core interaction sequence and corrected major navigation uncertainty.

Medium-Fi Prototype

Refined hierarchy, wording, and controls to support faster in-session decisions.

High-Fi Prototype

Polished interaction details and aligned visual language with product goals.

Testing Results

Measured task success and identified final usability opportunities before final report.

Prototype

Interactive Demo and Task Flows

Open Prototype in Figma

The prototype focuses on short decision windows in climbing sessions: discover route context, generate personalized beta, and execute with guidance.

Simple Task: Find and watch a community beta

Home → choose gym → open community beta → play video.

Moderate Task: Generate a personalized beta

Capture/upload route → select holds → input body data → generate beta.

Complex Task: Follow guided climbing mode

Start guided mode → navigate steps with previous/next → exit session safely.
